# People & devices

Under **Collaboration** you find the tabs **Overview**, **People & devices**, **Invites**, **Roles** and **Emergency**. Only the **board** may invite people, change roles and remove devices.

## Inviting a person

1. **Who and which role:** the person's name and their role (board, treasurer, secretary, coach, equipment manager). Open ClubManager shows what the role may do.
2. **Folder access:** give the person write access to the shared folder at your cloud provider. This isn't done in Open ClubManager, but you get step-by-step instructions for Nextcloud, OneDrive, Dropbox or Google Drive.
3. **Invite code:** send the person the code, e.g. by email or messenger. **Show e-mail text** gives you a ready-made text to copy.

The code is valid for **7 days** and works **once**. Open invites are listed in the **Invites** tab, where you can **withdraw** them.

## Approving a join request

When the invited person joins, a **join request** appears for you.

1. Call the person and ask for the **safety code** shown on their screen.
2. Enter it and click **Give access**.

If the code doesn't match, **reject the request**: someone else used the invite. After three wrong codes, the request is rejected automatically.

## Roles

The five roles are fixed:

| Role | May |
|---|---|
| **Board** | see and edit everything, invite others |
| **Treasurer** | edit bank details and finances, see members and contacts |
| **Secretary** | maintain members and contact details |
| **Coach** | see the member list and inventory |
| **Equipment manager** | edit the inventory, see the member list |

The full table is under [Users & roles](../erste-schritte/rollen.md#device-roles-in-collaboration).

### Changing a role

Under **People & devices → Change role**. If someone gets fewer permissions, they no longer see **new** data from the removed areas. What they have already seen stays on their device.

## Removing a device

If a device is lost or stolen, or someone leaves their position: **People & devices → Remove device**, and choose the reason.

Afterwards:

- The device receives **no new data**.
- Changes it makes from now on are **ignored** by everyone.
- What is **already** stored on the device cannot be deleted.

!!! danger "Loss or theft: report the data breach"
    If a device with member data gets lost, this may be a data breach that must be reported to the data protection authority within **72 hours** (Art. 33 GDPR). Disk encryption reduces the risk considerably.

You can't remove your own device. There must always be at least one device with the board role.
