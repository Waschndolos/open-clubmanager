# Overview (dashboard)

The overview is your start page after logging in.

![Overview](../assets/screenshots/dashboard.png){ .screenshot }

## What you see here

- **Key figures:** number of members (and how many are active), sections, groups, roles, and how many fees have been paid this year.
- **Quick access:** straight to members, a new transaction, the club structure or the recent changes.
- **Upcoming events** from the [calendar](termine.md).
- **Notice cards** whenever there is something to do:
    - My tasks (open and overdue): see [Tasks](aufgaben.md)
    - Upcoming anniversaries: see [Anniversaries & honors](ehrungen.md)
    - Expiring qualifications: see [Qualifications](qualifikationen.md)
    - Inspections & maintenance due: see [Inventory](inventar.md)
    - Households without a main payer: see [Families & households](haushalte.md)
- **Statistics & charts:** member development over the last 12 months, income and expenses, fee status and members per section.

Notice cards only appear when there really is something to show, and only for areas your role may see.
