# From local to collaboration

You have used Open ClubManager on your own so far, and now the board should work with it too. Your existing data is carried over.

## Procedure

1. **Create a backup.**
2. **Tidy up:** remove test data, merge duplicate members. Everything in the database now will be distributed to everyone.
3. **Collaboration → Turn on collaboration** and [set up the club](../zusammenarbeit/einrichten.md). The data on this device becomes the club's **starting state**.
4. **Print the emergency key** and store it safely.
5. [Invite](../zusammenarbeit/personen-geraete.md#inviting-a-person) the others.

!!! warning "Joining devices lose their local data"
    Anyone who joins and already has their own club data on their device (e.g. because they also used Open ClubManager on their own) receives the club's data, and their local data is **replaced** (a backup is created automatically). So merge several local datasets **beforehand** on the device that sets up the club, e.g. via [Excel export and import](von-excel.md).

## User accounts

User accounts from [user management](../funktionen/benutzer.md) only apply to this device and are **not** transferred. Each person logs in on their own device with their own account; what their device may see is decided by the [device role](../zusammenarbeit/personen-geraete.md#roles).

## Back to local?

Turning collaboration off is not supported at the moment. If you want to work alone again, take a backup of the current state and restore it on a device without collaboration, or export the members to Excel.
