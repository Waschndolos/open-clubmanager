# Open ClubManager

**Free club management software for your computer.** Members, fees, events, attendance and inventory: no subscription, no cloud account, no IT department needed.

![The overview page of Open ClubManager](assets/screenshots/dashboard.png){ .screenshot }

!!! warning "Still in development"
    Version 1.0 of Open ClubManager has not been released yet. Try out everything you like, but don't store real bank details in it until the first stable release is out.

## Who is Open ClubManager for?

Small and medium-sized clubs and associations that are run today with spreadsheets, word-processor templates and a lot of memory work: sports clubs, music societies, booster clubs, cultural associations. It is made for the volunteers who do the work: the board, the treasurer, the secretary, the coaches.

## What Open ClubManager is

- A **program for your computer** (Windows, macOS, Linux). You install it like any other program.
- **Free and open source**, permanently, with no member limit.
- **Local**: your data is stored in a file on your computer. Nobody else has access to it.
- **Works offline**: you only need the internet if you work together with others.
- **Usable as a team**: several board members can work together. The data is exchanged in encrypted form through a folder your club already has (Nextcloud, OneDrive, Dropbox, Google Drive, a network drive). See [Collaboration](zusammenarbeit/index.md).

## What Open ClubManager is not

- **Not a web application.** There is no website to log in to and no mobile app. Open ClubManager only runs as an installed program.
- **Not a member portal.** Members don't log in or maintain their own data.
- **Not tax accounting.** The cash book helps with fees, income and expenses, but it does not replace double-entry bookkeeping or your tax advisor.
- **Not a newsletter or chat tool.** You can export lists and calendars and use them with the programs you already have.
- **Not software for large federations** with many member clubs.

## Advantages and drawbacks at a glance

=== "Advantages"

    - No running costs, no subscription
    - Nothing technical to operate: no server, no database
    - Your data stays with you; cloud folders only ever see encrypted data
    - Works without internet, e.g. in the sports hall
    - Access rights are enforced technically: whoever may not see bank details never receives them
    - No lock-in: the data is an open SQLite file and can be exported at any time

=== "Drawbacks"

    - Every board member has to install the program on their own device and keep it up to date
    - No access from a phone or a browser
    - Members can't maintain their own data
    - When working together, other people's changes arrive with a short delay
    - You are responsible for your own backups
    - Collaboration comes with an emergency key that must be stored safely
